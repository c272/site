title: Transience and Digital Gardens
layout: note
date: 2026-05-16
place: Tokyo, Japan
---

I've been thinking a lot lately about the transience of the things I make and put out into the world.

A large portion of what I've created on the open web has bitrotted into unusability, shrivelled into a dead link, or disappeared entirely.

So, at least for my own small corner of the internet, I've decided to get my ducks in a row. Published links won't move or break, unlisted content will leave permalinks intact. Edits will be clearly marked.

With all that's going on at the moment, keeping my [digital garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history) sane is the least I can do, for myself.
